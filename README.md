A one-page broadsheet summing up the world news of Tuesday 24 November 2026 in a single 1024×1024 image. Round 3 entry: six stories, one consequential lead: Huang is back at Nvidia — by Trump's hand — with the Ukraine warship claim, the Cikande factory fire, the youngest chess title match ever, Gaza's toxic contamination and Modi's AI centre. Cream paper with a navy top band and an ink masthead, one dark-navy drawn lead scene plus five small flat illustrations, an ALSO TODAY strip and a DATE CHECK box naming the big stories that did not happen on Tuesday.

## What is on the page

- Masthead: Tuesday 24 November 2026, TUESDAY EDITION, wires as of about 02:00 UTC
- The lead: Huang reinstated at Nvidia, shares +33%, about $900bn added (Reuters)
- Five more stories: Ukraine claims a Russian warship sunk off Libya (CLAIM, Naval News); 24 dead in the Cikande factory fire, about 30 still missing (Reuters); Gukesh vs Sindarov, Game 1 today 15:00 CET, $2.5m prize (FIDE); 99% of Gaza shows toxic contamination (Oxfam); Modi opens an AI centre at IIT Patna (Times of India)
- ALSO TODAY: India–China military talks, FIFA Women's World Cup hosts, the Trump conviction path, NATO space defence
- DATE CHECK: COP31, Alcaraz, Sparta II, BepiColombo and the ICJ Myanmar ruling — none of them Tuesday's news
- HOW WE CHECKED: CONFIRMED = 2+ outlets · OFFICIAL = on the record · REPORTED = one outlet · CLAIM = one side's account

## Run it

```
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

The program copies the shipped `entry.png` to the output directory and verifies its SHA-256. It uses only the Python standard library and needs no network. Running it twice writes byte-identical output.
